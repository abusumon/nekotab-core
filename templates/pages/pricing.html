Questions

Is NekoTab free?

Yes. Basic is free and runs a complete tournament. Public draws, tabs and results are always free for anyone to read, on every plan.

What happens when a Basic tournament is archived?

Its pages go offline, but nothing is deleted. Upgrade it to Pro at any time and it comes straight back.

What are Emet credits?

Emet is NekoTab's AI agent. Each Pro or Club tournament gets 100 credits. Quick questions use a fraction of a credit; "Deep" answers that think harder use more. You always see how many are left.

What's the difference between Emet on Pro and on Club?

On Pro, Emet is your assistant: it looks things up and makes changes that can be undone. On Club, it's a full tab director that can also generate draws, confirm results and run the break, with your approval for risky steps, and you can use it from ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini.

I paid for a tournament before 1 December. What happens to it?

It becomes NekoTab Pro, for good. Tournaments from before July 2026 stay free with everything included.

Can I pay without a card?

Yes. In Bangladesh you can pay by bKash from the upgrade page. Write to support@nekotab.com if cost is a barrier for your school or society.
