{% trans "Why we started charging" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} NekoTab was free for its first six months. In that time more than 550 tournaments ran on it, and it made no money at all. We tried advertising; it earned a rounding error and made the site worse to use. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed with price=price_label %} The choice was to let NekoTab rot, or to ask for something. {{ price }} per tournament is what it takes to keep the servers running, the backups honest, and someone available when a draw breaks an hour before round one. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} We have set it deliberately low — well under what comparable tab software charges. It is a one-off, not a subscription, and it is per tournament, not per team. {% endblocktrans %}
