A Free CalicoTab Alternative for Debate Tournaments

CalicoTab is known for hosted Tabbycat with instant setup and predictable per-tournament pricing. NekoTab takes a different approach: free hosted debate tabulation plus a searchable motion bank, learning content, community tooling, and organization workspaces.

What CalicoTab Emphasizes

  • Instant hosted setup for tournaments
  • Built-in archiving and hosted infrastructure
  • Predictable per-tournament pricing
  • Automatic scaling without self-hosting work

Why Organizers Choose NekoTab Instead

  • One-off per-tournament cost, no subscription: see pricing. Public draws, tabs and results pages are always free to read, and there are no adverts.
  • Broader product surface: motion bank, learn hub, forum, and organization workspaces
  • International debate focus: BP, WSDC, Australs and Congress workflows
  • Open source: transparent codebase with self-hosting optionality
  • Motion research included: browse the NekoTab Motion Bank for prep and training

Moving Your Tournaments Across

Both platforms run Tabbycat, so a tournament’s full history moves between them as a standard Tabbycat XML archive. Export it from CalicoTab, upload it to NekoTab, and the teams, speakers, adjudicators, venues, motions, draw and every ballot and speaker score come with it. Migration is free and unlimited.

Two things worth knowing before you move: tournament settings are re-derived from the file rather than copied, so check your configuration afterwards; and participant private URLs are reissued here, since those are specific to the site that generated them.

You do the export yourself, signed in as an administrator of your own tournament, using CalicoTab’s own export feature. NekoTab never connects to CalicoTab and never asks for your credentials there.

Best Fit

If you want paid hosted Tabbycat with a simple pricing model, CalicoTab has a clear positioning. If you want motion discovery, public content, and open-source flexibility alongside your tabulation, NekoTab is the stronger long-term platform.
