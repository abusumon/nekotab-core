{% comment %} Mid-table units. Draw, standings, tab and results pages hold readers for 90–200s of scrolling through one long table; a single unit above the fold only ever collects one viewable impression however long they stay. This injects units *into* the table body and repeats them down its length, so scrolling keeps bringing fresh inventory into view. Tabbycat renders most tables through Vue, so the DOM is not ready at parse time — hence the observer with a hard timeout. If no table long enough turns up, nothing renders at all. The same ad unit id is reused for every insertion. AdSense permits one unit to appear many times on a page; reporting aggregates them, which is why the four slots are split by *position* rather than by count.
